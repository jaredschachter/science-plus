Recording

These sessions were held in person, before Science Plus moved online, and were not recorded. There is a powerpoint from the 2017 presentation, “Mathematicians are Different,” and the link to it will be posted here.

About this session

Longtime follower of Science Plus and Emeritus Professor of Mathematics, UBC. Fred presented to Science Plus on two occasions: “Mathematicians are Different” on April 26, 2017, and a second presentation in May 2019, whose slide deck is preserved.
