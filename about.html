About Science Plus

A community of curious minds.

Science Plus is not a formal institution. It's a gathering — warm, informal, and driven by genuine intellectual curiosity.

"The usual eclectic mixture of science, politics and history."

Dear Colleagues — that's how every Science Plus invitation begins. Not because it's formal, but because that's what we are: colleagues in the broadest sense. People who share a belief that the world is better understood than not, and that understanding it together is one of the great pleasures of being alive.

Science Plus began in 2020 as a simple idea: gather a community of engaged, curious people and introduce them to some of the most extraordinary minds working today. What surprised us was how quickly it became something more — a shared conversation stretching across sessions, across years, across fields of human inquiry.

How it works

Every session takes place on Zoom. Ray Schachter personally selects each guest and interviews them in a format that's closer to a conversation than a lecture — informal, wide-ranging, with room for questions. The goal is never to summarize a field but to open a door into it.

Past guests have included neuroscientists, theoretical physicists, political economists, volcanologists, philosophers of science, diplomats, and AI researchers. The thread connecting them is not a single discipline but a shared seriousness about ideas and a genuine desire to be understood by people outside their specialty.

Recordings of many past sessions are available to attendees on request — a growing archive of conversations that reward revisiting.

Ray's vision

Ray Schachter is a Vancouver-based lawyer whose intellectual life has always refused to stay inside professional boundaries. He reads widely, thinks historically, and brings to each session a host's most valuable quality: genuine curiosity about the guest in front of him.

He introduces each speaker with warmth and deep context — not as a moderator managing the clock, but as someone who has actually engaged with the ideas and wants to share that engagement with the room.

"Being introduced to the best humanity has to offer — not only in science, but also in culture, music and literature — gives life an existential purpose and gives us hope of a better road ahead."

Ray Schachter, Organizer

Who attends

The Science Plus community draws from academia, law, medicine, the arts, and business. What members share is not a background but an orientation — a belief that engaging seriously with ideas from outside one's own field is not a luxury but a necessity.

Sessions are small enough to feel intimate. Questions are encouraged. The tone is collegial rather than hierarchical. Nobody needs credentials to participate — only curiosity.
