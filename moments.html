Moments from Science Plus

Words that change how you see the world.

Every session leaves something behind. These are the phrases, ideas, and observations that have stayed with us — curated by Ray Schachter from six years of conversations.

"

Being introduced to the best humanity has to offer — in science, culture, music, and literature — gives life an existential purpose and gives us hope of a better road ahead.

Ray Schachter
Organizer · On Science Plus's purpose
The sentence Ray returns to when explaining why he runs this series. It's not about credentials. It's about what it feels like to be genuinely awestruck.
"

We are in the midst of a rupture, not a transition.

Mark Carney
Quoted by Ray Schachter · Andrew Standley Session, January 2026
Ray opened the Standley session with this line. Seven words that reframe everything happening in global politics right now.
"

Some of the most intelligent and dedicated scientists in the world have spent much of their careers on this issue alone. We care because we are curious — and unification would confirm our fundamental beliefs that we can and will truly understand nature.

Ray Schachter
Introducing Bianca Dittrich · December 2025
On why loop quantum gravity deserves a room full of non-physicists paying close attention.
"

This reminds me of the joke about fusion — it is 20 years away and always will be.

Ray Schachter
Introducing Robert Raussendorf · October 2025
Ray's light touch with a genuinely deep point: technological revolutions are harder to predict than we want them to be.
"

The prospect of civilizations on Mars emerged with the observation of 'canals' seen from earth — eerily prescient when we see photos of the surface from Perseverance.

Ray Schachter
Introducing Graziella Caprarelli · January 2026
History and planetary science braided together in a single sentence. The best kind of Science Plus introduction.
"

Even if the technical part of presentations was difficult, the feeling of awe listening to these folks was palpable.

Ray Schachter
Reflecting on the Quantum Series
This is what Science Plus is really for. You don't need to understand every equation to be moved by what's at stake.
"

The world is at a crossroads. In the past Science Plus has invited presentations on politics, geopolitics, social science and the environment. Responding to this current and existential crisis, we are fortunate indeed to have Andrew as a guest.

Ray Schachter
Introducing Andrew Standley · January 2026
Science Plus has always understood that the political and the scientific are not separate realms. This session was about why that matters now more than ever.
"

Subjects such as Large Language Models, quantum computers and Loop Quantum Gravity are notoriously difficult to present accessibly to non-scientists — but the 2025 season rose to the challenge.

Ray Schachter
Year-end letter · December 2025
A quiet statement of pride — and an acknowledgment of what the series actually asks of its speakers: not just expertise, but generosity.
"

Science and culture are being attacked almost daily. The Planetary Society was formed by Carl Sagan and Bruce Murray in 1980. They saw enormous public interest in space that was not reflected by government investment.

Ray Schachter
September 2025
Ray drawing a line from Sagan's era to ours — a reminder that the fight for science in public life is not new, and that curiosity is itself a form of resistance.
