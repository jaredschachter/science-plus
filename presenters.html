The minds behind the conversations.

Scientists, mathematicians, physicians, geneticists, economists, diplomats and philosophers who have joined Ray Schachter for conversation since 2013 — around a living room until 2020, and online ever since.

Mark Bernstein
Neurosurgery and Medical Ethics
June 2026
A Toronto academic neurosurgeon of more than forty years, he has studied surgical error across his own 2000–2019 caseload, practised awake craniotomy, and helped build neurosurgical capacity in more than fifteen lower-income countries. His three A's for surgeons who err: acknowledge, acquire knowledge, ask forgiveness.
