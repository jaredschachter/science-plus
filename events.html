Statistics & AI

Larry Wasserman — What Is Statistics, What Is It Used For, and How Has AI Influenced It?

📹 Online · Free to Attend

Larry Wasserman, UPMC Professor of Statistics and Data Science at Carnegie Mellon University and a member of the National Academy of Sciences, returns to Science Plus. He first presented in 2014, when the series was just beginning, on Bayesian statistics at Ray’s home. A lot has happened since, and Larry has been in the thick of it.

His work is on the mathematical foundations of statistics and machine learning, and the question underneath both: what can we legitimately conclude from data, and how certain should we be that our conclusions are correct? Science, economics and public policy all rest on statistical results. So, increasingly, does artificial intelligence — a machine-learning system may make an impressive prediction, but statistical theory tells us how reliable that prediction is, what assumptions it depends on, and when it might fail.

Larry knows the group is non-expert and he is a great communicator, so expect an accessible and rewarding presentation. This is the last session of 2026.
