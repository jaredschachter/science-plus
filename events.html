Environmental Science

Sandy Steffen — Arctic Mercury Pollution and the 2025 Antarctic Expedition

📹 Online · Free to Attend

Alexandra (Sandy) Steffen, Senior Mercury Researcher at Environment and Climate Change Canada, on her work on mercury — from collecting in the Arctic, to her work with the UN and the international treaty for the reduction of global mercury, to her global program and the 2025 project in the Antarctic — and why contaminants in ecosystems matter for climate change, ecosystem health, and people relying on country foods in the north. She is also happy to talk about being a woman in science and the challenges she has faced and overcome.

How It Works

Simple, intimate, serious.
