Father and Daughter

Charles Beichman presented on the James Webb Space Telescope in February 2022. In March 2023 his daughter Annabel Beichman presented on the genomes of endangered marine mammals. Thirteen months apart, to the same salon.

Some of the Presenters

A few of the presenters.

Drawn from the full list on the presenters page.

Physics
October 2020

Leonard Susskind

Stanford's Felix Bloch Professor of Theoretical Physics, one of the founders of string theory and the holographic principle, on string theory, black holes and the landscape, in an open Q&A.

Climate & Environment
October 2019

Suzanne Simard

The UBC ecologist behind Finding the Mother Tree explained how trees communicate and share resources through underground fungal networks.

Biology & Evolution
January 2023

Neil Shubin

University of Chicago paleontologist, co-discoverer of Tiktaalik — the "fish with hands" — and author of Your Inner Fish, which gave the session its name.

Medicine & Genomics
December 2022

Michael Hayden

A world authority on the genetics of Huntington's disease, UBC's Michael Hayden made the case that no disease is hopeless anymore.

Medicine & Genomics
Nov. 2020 & May 2017

Marco Marra

Director of the BC Cancer Agency Genome Sciences Centre, whose team sequenced the SARS coronavirus genome in 2003, spoke twice — on cancer genomics and the Personalized OncoGenomics Project.

Physics
October 2021

Neil Turok

The former Director of the Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics ranged from personal history to the shape of the universe, and his work building science and mathematics capacity in Africa.

Philosophy
June 2022

Rebecca Goldstein

Philosopher and author, who spoke on Plato at the Googleplex and on the drive that propels human beings to want to matter.

Geopolitics
June 2025

Janice Stein

Founding Director of the Munk School of Global Affairs & Public Policy, a Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada and member of the Order of Canada, on Putin and world politics.

Physics
September 2022

Michel Laberge

Founder of General Fusion, Burnaby, BC, on the company's progress and the wider global race for commercial fusion power.

Space & Planets
Feb. 2022 & Mar. 2023

Charles & Annabel Beichman

Charles presented on the James Webb Space Telescope weeks after its launch. His daughter Annabel presented a year later on the genomes of endangered marine mammals.
