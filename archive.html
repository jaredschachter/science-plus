Recordings Archive

The conversations, preserved.

Recordings of many past Science Plus sessions exist and are available to attendees. If you attended a session and would like access, or if you're new and want to catch up — just get in touch.

A growing library of conversations

Science Plus has gathered since 2013. In the early years the record is a library of presentations and slides; when sessions moved online in 2020, full recordings began to be preserved. Together they reflect more than a decade of intellectual life. Listening back to a conversation with Leonard Susskind or Marco Marra feels different the second time, with more context, more questions of your own.

Recordings are available to members of the Science Plus community when presenters have consented to publication. The same is true of PowerPoint presentations.

How to request access

If you'd like to watch a recording from a past session, use the form below. Ray will follow up personally. Specify the session or speaker you're looking for, or simply say you'd like to be added to the community list. You can also email Ray directly at schachter.ray@gmail.com.

✓ Your email app should open — press send and it reaches Ray directly.
