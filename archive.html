Recordings Archive

The conversations, preserved.

Recordings of many past Science Plus sessions exist and are available to attendees. If you attended a session and would like access, or if you're new and want to catch up — just get in touch.

A growing library of conversations

Since Science Plus began in 2020, recordings of many sessions have been preserved — a quiet archive that reflects six years of intellectual life. Listening back to a conversation with Leonard Susskind or Suzanne Simard feels different the second time, with more context, more questions of your own.

Recordings are available to members of the Science Plus community. They're not hosted publicly — this is a gathering, not a broadcast — but access is available on request.

How to request access

If you'd like to watch a recording from a past session, use the form below. Ray will follow up personally. Specify the session or speaker you're looking for, or simply say you'd like to be added to the community list.
